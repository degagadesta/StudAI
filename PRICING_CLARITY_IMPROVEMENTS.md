## 🎯 Clear Pricing Communication

### What Users Now Understand:

1. **Monthly vs Yearly**:
   - Monthly: Pay each month, more flexible
   - Yearly: Pay once, save 20-33%

2. **Feature Limits**:
   - FREE: Clear monthly limits
   - STUDENT: Generous limits for regular use
   - PRO: No limits at all

3. **Value Proposition**:
   - Each plan clearly states who it's for
   - Features match the target user's needs
   - Upgrade path is obvious

4. **Savings Calculation**:
   - Student monthly: 149 × 12 = 1,788 ETB
   - Student yearly: 1,200 ETB
   - **You save: 588 ETB (33%)**
   
   - Pro monthly: 249 × 12 = 2,988 ETB
   - Pro yearly: 2,400 ETB
   - **You save: 588 ETB (20%)**
